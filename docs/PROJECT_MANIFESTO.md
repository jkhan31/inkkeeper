# InkKeeper Product Manifesto: The Quiet Archive

InkKeeper exists to help readers remember the ideas that stayed with them after reading. 

Most reading tools track consumption: books finished, pages read, or reading streaks. **InkKeeper captures the moment an idea lands.**

## 1. The Core Ritual
Every feature in InkKeeper must support one simple behavior:
**Finish reading ↓ Pause ↓ Capture the idea that stayed.**

This moment of articulation transforms reading from passive consumption into lasting insight. If a feature weakens this ritual, it should not exist.

## 2. Product Philosophy

### Reflection Over Tracking
InkKeeper does not measure performance. There are no streaks, leaderboards, or productivity metrics. The product focuses on thinking, not "doing."

### One Thought Is Enough
Each reading moment produces one reflection. Not five. Not a summary. Just the idea that lingered. This keeps the ritual light and repeatable.

### Ideas Before Metadata
The reflection is the primary object; everything else is context. The UI should always emphasize the idea first.

### Calm Design
InkKeeper should feel like a quiet reading room. The interface avoids noise, gamification, and clutter. Users should feel still when they open the app.

### Rediscovery Over Review
InkKeeper does not ask users to “review notes.” Instead, it gently resurfaces past reflections: *“You once wrote this.”* This transforms the archive from storage into memory.

## 3. Capture Modes

### Reading Sessions (Structured)
A deliberate practice: **Start session ↓ Read ↓ End session ↓ Capture reflection.**

### Quick Capture (Spontaneous)
Ideas often appear outside a tracked session (e.g., after closing a physical book). **Quick Capture** ensures ideas are never lost due to friction.

### Widget Capture (Instant)
Future home screen widgets exist solely to make reflection effortless. They should never include browsing, analytics, or session features.

## 4. The Design System

### Color Palette
* **Rice Paper (`#FAF5F0`)** → Background
* **Sumi Ink (`#1A1A1A`)** → Primary Text
* **Seal Rust (`#8F270D`)** → Primary Actions
* **Sage (`#4D6B57`)** → Subtle interface states

### Typography & Geometry
* **Typography:** Prioritize clarity, spacious line heights, and a literary feel (Serif).
* **Geometry:** Avoid harsh edges. 
    * **Cards:** Rounded (`2rem`)
    * **Buttons:** Rounded-full
    * **Inputs:** Soft rounded edges

### Motion
Animation must be subtle (fades and soft transitions). No bouncing effects or flashy "celebratory" UI feedback.

## 5. What InkKeeper Is Not
InkKeeper is **not** a reading tracker, a highlight manager (like Readwise), a knowledge graph (like Obsidian), or a productivity dashboard. It captures moments of **intellectual impact.**

## 6. Product Guardrails
Every feature must pass three questions:
1. Does this make reflection easier? 
2. Does this keep the product calm? 
3. Does this emphasize ideas over data?
