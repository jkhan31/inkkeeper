# InkKeeper Product Manifesto: The Quiet Archive

InkKeeper exists to help readers remember the ideas that stayed with them after reading. 

Most reading tools track consumption: books finished, pages read, or reading streaks. **InkKeeper captures the moment an idea lands.**

## 1. The Core Ritual
Every feature in InkKeeper must support one simple behavior:
**Finish reading ↓ Pause ↓ Capture the idea that stayed.**

This moment of articulation transforms reading from passive consumption into lasting insight. If a feature weakens this ritual, it should not exist.

## 2. Product Philosophy

### Reflection Over Tracking
InkKeeper does not measure performance. There are no streaks, leaderboards, or productivity metrics. The product focuses on thinking, not "doing."

### One Thought Is Enough
Each reading moment produces one reflection. Not five. Not a summary. Just the idea that lingered. This keeps the ritual light and repeatable.

### Ideas Before Metadata
The reflection is the primary object; everything else is context. The UI should always emphasize the idea first.

### Calm Design
InkKeeper should feel like a quiet reading room. The interface avoids noise, gamification, and clutter. Users should feel still when they open the app.

### Rediscovery Over Review
InkKeeper does not ask users to “review notes.” Instead, it gently resurfaces past reflections: *“You once wrote this.”* This transforms the archive from storage into memory.

## 3. Capture Modes

### Reading Sessions (Structured)
A deliberate practice: **Start session ↓ Read ↓ End session ↓ Capture reflection.**

### Quick Capture (Spontaneous)
Ideas often appear outside a tracked session (e.g., after closing a physical book). **Quick Capture** ensures ideas are never lost due to friction.

### Widget Capture (Instant)
Future home screen widgets exist solely to make reflection effortless. They should never include browsing, analytics, or session features.

## 4. The Design System

### Color Palette
* **Rice Paper (`#FAF5F0`)** → Background
* **Sumi Ink (`#1A1A1A`)** → Primary Text
* **Seal Rust (`#8F270D`)** → Primary Actions
* **Sage (`#4D6B57`)** → Subtle interface states

### Typography & Geometry
* **Typography:** Prioritize clarity, spacious line heights, and a literary feel (Serif).
* **Geometry:** Avoid harsh edges. 
    * **Cards:** Rounded (`2rem`)
    * **Buttons:** Rounded-full
    * **Inputs:** Soft rounded edges

### Motion
Animation must be subtle (fades and soft transitions). No bouncing effects or flashy "celebratory" UI feedback.

## 5. What InkKeeper Is Not
InkKeeper is **not** a reading tracker, a highlight manager (like Readwise), a knowledge graph (like Obsidian), or a productivity dashboard. It captures moments of **intellectual impact.**

## 6. Product Guardrails
Every feature must pass three questions:
1. Does this make reflection easier? 
2. Does this keep the product calm? 
3. Does this emphasize ideas over data?

# InkKeeper Project Manifesto 
## The Quiet Archive 
InkKeeper is a personal thinking archive for readers. It exists to capture the ideas that stay with you after reading. 

Most reading tools track **consumption**: 
- books finished 
- pages read 
- reading streaks 

InkKeeper tracks something different: **the ideas that stayed with you.** 
--- 
# Core Ritual 
InkKeeper revolves around a single moment. 
Finish reading → pause → capture the idea that stayed This is the core loop of the product.

Read ↓ Reflect ↓ Archive ↓ Rediscover later

Every feature must support this ritual. If a feature interrupts or complicates this moment, it should not exist. 

--- 
# The Philosophy of Restraint 
InkKeeper is intentionally minimal. The product avoids: 
- streaks 
- gamification 
- productivity pressure 
- knowledge-management complexity 

The goal is not to manage information. The goal is to **remember ideas**. 

--- 
# One Idea Per Reflection 
InkKeeper captures **one idea at a time**. Not summaries. Not notes. Not highlights. Just the single idea that stayed with you. The interface reinforces this constraint. 

Example prompt: > What stayed with you? Reflection input: > The idea that stayed with me was… 
Helper text: > One idea is enough. This constraint reduces friction and improves reflection quality. 

--- 
# Reflection Over Metadata 
The reflection is always the most important element. Hierarchy: 
1. Reflection text 
2. Book title 
3. Date 
4. Session duration Metadata exists only to provide context. 

The idea itself is the hero. 

--- 
# Reading Flexibility 
InkKeeper supports two reflection paths. ## Reading Session A quiet timer for focused reading.

Start session ↓ Read ↓ End session ↓ Capture reflection

## Quick Capture 
Capture an idea without a session. Used when reading: 
- articles 
- physical books 
- ebooks 
- audiobooks 

Quick capture ensures reflections are never lost simply because the timer was not used. 
--- 
# Rediscovery 
Ideas should not disappear into storage. InkKeeper periodically resurfaces past reflections. Example:

From Your Archive
"Systems beat motivation."
Atomic Habits
2 months ago

Rediscovery transforms the archive from **storage** into **memory**. These moments create emotional connection with the archive. 

--- 
# The Archive 
The archive is not a database. It is a **personal library of ideas**. Reflections are organized chronologically, grouped by month. Over time the archive reveals: 
- books that shaped your thinking 
- ideas that keep returning 
- patterns in what you notice while reading The archive should feel like browsing a quiet library of your own thoughts. 

--- 
# Calm Interface 
InkKeeper should feel like **a quiet reading room at dawn**. Design principles: 
- minimal interface 
- generous whitespace 
- slow pacing 
- no visual noise Typography hierarchy should emphasize:

Idea ↓ Book ↓ Metadata

Each screen should have **one clear intention**. Dashboard → start reading or capture an idea Session → read Reflection → capture the idea Archive → revisit ideas --- # Progressive Meaning The value of InkKeeper increases as the archive grows. The interface evolves gradually. Early stage:
simple archive

Later:
rediscovery

Later still:
insights about the archive

Meaning should **emerge naturally over time**. Features should never appear before the archive justifies them. 

--- 
# What InkKeeper Is Not 
InkKeeper is not: 
- a reading tracker 
- a note-taking system 
- a knowledge management tool 
- a productivity dashboard It is a **reflection ritual**. 

--- 
# Long-Term Vision 
Over years the archive becomes something rare: a record of how your thinking evolved through reading. Users can see: 
- ideas that shaped them 
- books that influenced them 
- themes that appeared in their reflections InkKeeper becomes a **personal intellectual history**. 

--- 
# Guiding Question 
Whenever a new feature is proposed, ask: > Does this help capture, rediscover, or understand ideas? If not, it probably does not belong in InkKeeper.
