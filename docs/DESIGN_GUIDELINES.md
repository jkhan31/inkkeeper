# InkKeeper — Design Guidelines

## Emotional Thesis

Feels like a quiet reading room at dawn — grounded, restrained, softly lit.

Not motivational.
Not energetic.
Not playful.

---

## Typography

Sans-serif.
Neutral.
Soft.

Scale:

H1: 28px  
H2: 22px  
H3: 18px  
Body: 16px  
Caption: 13px  
Timer: 48–64px (tabular numbers)

Line height ≥ 1.5  
No aggressive bold.  
No uppercase shouting.

---

## Color System

Rice Paper: #FAF5F0  
Soft Dark Rice: #F3EEE8  
Sumi Ink: #1A1A1A  
Deep Moss (Primary): #3F5A4A  
Muted Sage: #6A7F70  

Rules:

- Green only for primary CTA.
- Active mode background only during session.
- No gradients.
- No dark mode (v0.1).

WCAG AA compliant.

---

## Spacing

8pt grid.
24px mobile padding.
Max width: 640px.

Whitespace is structural.

---

## Motion

150–250ms.
Ease-out.
No bounce.
No celebration.
No confetti.

---

## Voice & Tone

Measured.
Minimal.
Non-performative.

Examples:

Begin when ready.  
15 minutes selected.  
Session logged.  

No exclamation marks.

---

## Emotional Audit

Before shipping:

- Does this feel calm?
- Does this create pressure?
- Is this reinforcing ritual?
- Is anything visually loud?

If unsure, simplify.
